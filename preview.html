Questions

Frequently Asked

How long is the bus trip from Dammam to Makkah?

Usually 14 to 16 hours, depending on traffic, weather and rest stops.

Do you offer family and women’s packages?

Yes. We run family, group and women’s Umrah packages with family-friendly hotels and safe, comfortable transport.

Which routes do you run?

Dammam to Makkah departs daily. Dammam to Madinah departs every Sunday, Wednesday and Thursday.

Is Ziyarat included?

Most packages include Ziyarat in Makkah and Madinah, depending on the plan you choose.

What is your cancellation policy?

Refunds are available for cancellations within the stated period, and a fee may apply. Ask us for details when booking.
